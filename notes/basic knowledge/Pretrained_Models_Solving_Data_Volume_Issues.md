## Pretrained

This is the most important step to understand transfer learning.

Suppose there is an AI company that can recognize animals.

They have already spent:

```text
14 million images
Trained for half a year
```

And trained a very powerful model.

For example:

```text
ImageNet

Dog
Cat
Car
Airplane
Flower
Fruit
......

1000 classes
14 million images
```

After training, this model has already learned:

- Edges
- Textures
- Circles
- Colors
- Noses
- Eyes
- Ears

These are all very universal image features.

This is called a

> **Pretrained Model**
